Glass Walls, Briarhearts

A poetry collection.

R. Val

— — — — —

An introduction is a call to defense,

Who am I? Why write this down?

Why is this worth your time?

I’m not the one who can answer that.

In the following text, I will attempt to communicate something.

The shape and tenor is unknown to me,

As it is to you.

But It is within me and I need it to not be.

It will take the form of deception.

Not lies, no.

Refractions,

Details cast through prisms and reshaped.

You will see what you will.

You, me, some third party.

It will be true enough.

Humans are creatures of narrative.

1979, The Last Age

In murky mornings you sing

The song of those who came before.

By marsh, river, and sea,

Eons long endured to put you

In your appointed place.

~

So many once sang,

Kindred steadfast and true.

Less now, soon none.

Just you,

And the tune that ends all things.

~

Do you sing for another?

One like you, dusky and mottled.

Do you sing for their ghosts?

That you might tear the veil.

Do you sing because it is you?

And that is all you know how to do.

~

A last cry to breach expanse so vast.

You carry them on your wings.

Your brothers did too,

Gone now and soon you.

But let the coasts know,

Of the sparrows that were once here.

To the Unknown Assailant Currently Residing in my Bedroom

What did you feel,

That terrible day?

When you found yourself

Trapped in half washed locks?

The forest of some giant,

Careless and distant.

Thrash and pull,

anything to break free.

You cried for help,

For kin or god,

None heard you.

I didn’t help you,

I didn’t know,

I didn’t understand,

I’m sorry.

Would ask what you think,

But you don’t think, do you?

A life of hunger, rage, and fear.

It’s still a life.

Must’ve been deranged and miserable,

When I pulled you out.

Never even saw more than a glimpse.

Black carapace,

Soft and warm.

You clung to my hand so tight,

When you stung me.

Please leave.

In Memory of Private Riley Jacobs, 987–1011

“Fearfully and wonderfully made.”

True for some,

Not you.

Doomed as soon as you walk on screen.

~

The shots start,

You’re already gone.

Story cut short,

That others would be told.

~

No way out,

No path forward.

A prop in the dance,

Of rook and lion.

~

Did you know?

Scared to hell.

Did it hurt?

The heat in your side.

~

Did your mother dream for you?

A hero of old,

Dashing and bold,

Draped in violet.

~

A whole life lived,

A whole life wasted,

On a connecting paragraph,

And a grim fucking punchline.

~

Your path unfolded,

All roads lead to

Your appointed place.

The boots of your betters.

~

Ask me why,

Beg that this fate

may pass from you.

Another chance to be great.

~

Find no mercy,

For I am the Lord your God.

Might justifies itself,

And is an architect of ruin.

~

But let someone weep for the forgotten,

For the spaces filled in

With bright eyed innocents,

Cut down in the afterthought.

Lagoonborn

Your love has no price,

Not the blood cost of before.

Celebrated me,

my demented dance.

Tell me dear,

I may wade in your waters a while longer.

Make me believe,

Make me wish to see,

The world through amber eyes.

A world of hope and beauty,

that pales in your wake,

That I did not know existed before you.

Heavens bless those who bless you,

Hells take those who do you harm.

Guide my hands,

May I be half the woman

You always deserved.

This road will end, in parting or the grave,

As all roads do.

But for as long as I am able,

I wish to be yours true.

Draconic 11

So lick your wounds,

And I’ll keep watch.

Tell me your stories,

Pretend it’s the first time.

~

Magnets of like charge repulsed,

Yet we are the same.

Players of the dark game.

Practicers of sanguine rituals.

~

Never to align,

Sworn to destroy,

That which each other holds dear.

But what do we really believe in?

~

Brought together by two choices,

Sustained by dozens after.

A friendship,

An imploding submarine.

~

So go around again,

One more time.

We could get it right.

Just a dream, not always bad.

~

So stay away,

Wish you well, curse me to hell.

When the world grovels at your feet,

I’ll be a true believer.

The Watcher

Another day, what should be the aftermath.

A number on a screen tells me you’re still here.

Still here, above and beyond, out of reach.

I should feel safe, my guardian angel, but you’re no protector.

Your hate oozes down my spine, sickening and foul.

“Am I as hideous as you say?”

I type the thought, close the screen, and move on.

You’ll be there when I’m back, you’ll always be there.

What did I do to deserve this curse?

What did you do to be bound in kind?

That whenever I act, you watch.

Still here, still here.

7–11, 9:27 PM

I fear you,

stranger in my way.

Made to share moment brief,

To share this space too small.

~

I fear you,

Eyes I cannot read.

A smile hollow,

Is that a smile?

~

I hate you,

Enemy, alien, unknown.

Seed of Esau,

Born of adversity.

~

I love you,

Fellow wandering soul.

Human, same as me.

More alike than not.

<

I weep for you,

Child of the Last Age.

Swallowed by that cold maw,

Shoulder to shoulder.

~

I hope for you,

Your joys and triumphs.

That you bring beauty,

Into a world grown dark.

~

I fear me,

What you must see.

Half mad and scourged,

Less than once was.

~

I fear you,

Thoughts a mystery.

Will you kill me?

Would that be so bad?

~

I fear you.

~

I fear you.

Try again,

Step 1.

Step 1, Step 2,

Step 1,

Step 1,

Step 1,

Step 1,

Step 1,

Step 1,

Maybe the problem is you.

Breathe.

Breathe again,

Become an instrument.

Fingers at the keys.

Type,

Step 1,

Step 2,

Step 3,

Running again.

Poetry Won’t Fix This

Another skin,

Freshly shorn,

Newly worn.

Do this so easy,

Call a stranger’s face your own,

Never at home when you’re alone.

Play the part,

Actor,

Impostor,

Changeling.

Another way to escape yourself.

Another hat,

A costume change,

It won’t save you.

The rot’s still there,

Festering in bone.

You already know,

Do it for the art,

Play, pretend, perfect.

Getting so good at this,

Find another way.

Repeat, refresh

An answer for a time.

Another narrative,

To justify the void.

The Last Verse of Runner

Sticking your nose in bear traps

Like it never went out of style.

Another tragic girlie,

And her solipsistic deathwish.

Daring, are we?

~

Moths wouldn’t soar to flames,

The way you do

When you need to hurt.

~

What will it be?

A new drug habit?

A new mean brunette?

Night driving, headlights off?

Skip it, open your veins.

~

Say it makes you feel alive,

Still as dead as ever.

Say it’s for the art.

A single success at the foot

Of a mountain of waste.

~

Say it’s to be punished.

Cry me a river.

The sins of your father

won’t wash with blood.

~

Go on then,

What a life.

You’re no victim.

You’re holding the knife.

Scorpio, Gemini, Leo

We are travelers,

You and I,

And for a while

We walked the same road.

~

We were doomed,

Always to part,

And we are doomed still,

By weakness of flesh.

~

Find me lacking,

I’m not her,

I never could be.

Detached, reflective, prevaricatory.

~

Strike at me,

Make me hurt,

Make me bleed,

Again! Again!

~

But you who have no respect,

For sacred ground you defile.

You who walk into hallowed halls

Of another woman’s ruin.

~

I’m no better,

Ignored the wound in your side.

I saw the blood, fresh and gushing,

Said it wasn’t my place.

~

Still we lingered,

Could we have been sisters?

Told me we might never end;

I can spot a liar.

~

Gone the first chance you got,

Tender moments dissolved in rain.

My fault or not,

Does it matter?

~

You watch from your distance.

Are you audience, spectator?

Are you guardian, protector?

Are you judgment, damnation?

~

I feel you still,

In the glow of moonlight,

In songs curated,

In the tales I saw in your eyes.

~

Come and sit by the fire,

Tell me your stories.

The night is young,

And for now so are we.

~

Told you to find me if you need me,

Promised I’d help.

Look me up and find I’m a liar,

I have nothing for you.

In Lavender Dreams

No heart like yours,

None could ever compare.

Zealous faith of the reverent chosen.

Yearning for the promise of what will be.

A bird so used to bleeding,

Pouring out herself.

Joined by hound,

Ever faithful companion.

Should we linger a while longer,

See the shapes we take,

The lives we live,

The heart we share.

The traveler walks sandy dunes,

Thirsty and desperate,

drops to a crawl,

Spared by the oasis she finds.

The relief you give so freely.

So guard this place,

Steady shield and raise spear.

Never yield this gift,

The love you have given me.

Lurasidone, 80mg Daily

Behold, the great leviathan rises!

Its deep slumber halted,

To ravage vessels sailor-laden,

That the shores may run red.

A beast massive, mad, and manic,

The mother of monsters sings a song,

In tongue older than empires,

Crazed truths that ring through eternity.

Cast aside ploughshare, sword, fishhook,

The might of the sea will have its way.

Think not of love, mercy, or grace,

The serpent will obey only Yahweh.

~

The ancient demon once bound,

By chemical shackles,

Freed to wound the soul of woman,

To thrash and claw at mental walls.

Starved and thirsty,

It will have its fill,

And it will destroy all you hold dear,

But it will be with you still.

That companion never quite free,

It waited for so long, it missed you,

A mind to bend, to direct toward glory,

Is it so wrong to give in?

Notes on Nullification Procedure 1331

Who are you

When it all falls away?

Monks would kill for this tranquility,

But you don’t much feel a thing.

Music bleeds emotion,

All true, all true.

Sealed in the depths

Never to rise.

Will they ever see?

Will you?

Spark up,

Inhale too sharply,

Cough until you choke.

Laugh,

Because it’s all you can do.

Starved crops begging for the rain,

For when you finally cry

Abhor the Parasite

Limbs the roots of a vile weed.

Body bulbous and deformed.

Your voice raised from cursed flesh,

Soft, sweet, mismatched.

You’ll never be a woman looking like that.

Cry, seethe, joke, deflect,

They see what you are anyway.

Family says they love you,

Spit out the lie.

They love him, the thing that strangled you,

So you pushed him down, made to drown.

You had to, you had to.

You thought no one would blame you,

They mourned him and cursed your joy.

Get away, never look back.

Find escape in anything you can.

Smoke another cigarette and cough,

not your cough, but his.

Look in the mirror,

Not your face, but his.

Freak out,

Scratch your arms until they bleed,

Not your blood, but his.

You’re not your own,

This vessel isn’t big enough for two.

Try again to drown the parasite,

But is it him or you?

Hail, Haruspex

Scatter the bones,

Cast the entrails.

The blood sings

For the listening.

~

Speak true, speak power.

Glyphs conjured on flesh,

Seal fates and guide souls

Believers in your sight.

~

They don’t know,

You drew it from a dream.

Spun it with the thread,

Made it real.

~

Clutch the gutting knife,

It’s dirty work.

But you’re no watcher.

Vision cast upon the world.

~

Pay no mind,

To ichorous choirs.

You know best,

You have to.

~

To be wrong,

How could that be?

Unmoored, unshackled,

Adrift in the cosmic sea.

~

No, your purpose clear.

Your foremothers led you here.

Cast another fortune,

See only a reflection.

The Gnawing

Call me a liar,

Fraud,

Coward,

Loser,

Psycho.

~

All true

Would speak of you.

But how does the tide

Dream to describe the sea?

~

Such little true beauty left,

In such a bleak place.

It shone from you,

And I hid my face.

~

A tenfold thousand of hours,

Would not be enough,

To bask in the great depths

Of the oceans in your mind.

~

Took my hand, took my face,

Saw the hurt,

Said I must be tired,

Asked if I’d like to rest.

~

And lion lay with lamb

A love given freely

Unburdened by the cost of tears

And tax of pain.

~

That great many promises I swore

To be true to you.

Turned into a millstone

Wrapped around my neck.

~

To drown an oathbreaking whore.

~

Did you see it coming?

Signs in soul sickness made apparent.

Did you think this would be different?

Bless me with unworthy trust.

~

When my heart wandered,

It wasn’t you, rather your love

Squandered on a shard,

this sharp and brittle thing.

~

Didn’t go easy,

The stubborn push and pull.

Find another way,

This time it will work.

~

It has to.

~

It doesn’t, it won’t

Not your fault, mine.

Wished to be my everything,

But I’m still hungry.

~

The family dog howls,

Maggots infest old wounds.

Grab the rifle and a shell

We’ll do what we need to do.

But Seriously, Fuck You

Hit me like a freight train,

pretend it never happened.

Sigh it out or swallow it,

Gotta take it just the same.

The things we choose set out to sea

unfathomable waters of contingency.

~

Hold me in contempt

Of your infernal court.

Hold me like I’m poisonous,

Or keep my leash short.

At least you held me–

That’s enough, right?

~

Bring me your triumphs,

Set at my feet your woes.

I’ll howl your praises to the moon,

Because I’m your broken dog.

You never need ask

for that which I give freely.

~

So wash me with your hate,

carpet bomb with sweetness.

Mixed together icy hot

An agonizing sensation,

That would count as torture–

You should sell it to the CIA.

~

But I’m no victim,

Nor given to that weakness.

I chose this.

I choose this.

Call me worm,

Call me kindred.

~

This is a game,

And we’re both keeping score.

So forgive my psychosis

And I’ll absolve your psychopathy.

The sickness we share

Deep and cold as a cave.

~

Find your place,

In city lights or the shore,

I’ll remain here a while longer.

But watch out,

I’ve been spending time in the forest,

And I told the trees what you did.

Always More Blood

And lo that great temple has fallen!

Built not for a god but for

The shape of two.

It has stood before,

And it will stand again.

When tenderhearted pilgrims find refuge.

Not for love,

No, no, no,

But for kindred true.

Let another wanderer in,

Give them rest, give them care,

Moments shared, tragedy they precede.

Did you think it would be different,

Or did you pretend too?

Lies we tell to justify want,

Give unto this place its due.

Fed by sweetness and sorrow alike,

Their tales scrawled on walls to read.

There the High Priestess waits,

The next one comes in need.

She will always have more bones to break,

The white altar will remain sated,

She will always have more blood to bleed.

Title Track

Another crack,

The foundation strains.

Probably fine.

Words make the caulk

That seals it.

Good enough for now.

~

A whole life spent running,

Silver tongues buy time,

But there’s no end in sight.

~

Words.

Get troublesome girls hit.

Stay away from me.

~

Words.

Met with a read receipt.

What did I do?

~

Words.

Justify the narrative.

Keep them entertained.

~

Words.

Build the glass house

That lets them all see.

Find the right combination,

Make them understand.

They have to.

Alternatives unfathomable.

~

Crystal shards break,

Pierce skin,

Screams heard down the block.

A prison designed by captive,

Could never be happy anywhere.

~

In time,

She resembles her transparent tomb.

Sharp, hard, brittle.

A thorny thing.

Yet propelled by faith,

One day this will be over.

Flightless

I killed a bird today.

Didn’t mean to.

Doesn’t matter.

Dead the same.

What foul mockery,

To claim beak and wing.

False, grounded songbird,

Who prowls as Cain.

It stood on asphalt,

Tall and crying.

Wounded, stuck, trapped.

Couldn’t escape.

It called for mother,

It called for God,

It called for me,

None to hear.

To be the same,

Hearts belonging to the skies.

Seek a friend,

find an uncaring steel beast,

And the car I was driving.

Betrayer,

Brutus,

Calls myself your kin,

But just the spawn of Judas.

Seek remorse,

Seek anguish,

Seek conscience,

Find none left.

Because I’m not sorry,

Can’t feel much of anything.

But you’re gone.

And that’s my fault.

Life continues, one less.

Another ghost joins the choir,

But the birds remember.

The blood cries out,

The ink that forms words;

Each page a confession.
