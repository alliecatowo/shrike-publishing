## The Heist Deck: Sample Cards

The Heist Deck is a 52-card deck representing everything that can go wrong during a heist. Each card presents a complication, challenge, or unexpected situation the crew must navigate.

### Artwork Requirements

**Card Specifications:**
- Standard poker card size: 2.5 x 3.5 inches (63 x 88mm)
- Print resolution: 300 DPI (750 x 1050 pixels)
- Bleed area: 0.125 inches on all sides

**General Design Elements:**
- Consistent card back design (possibly featuring the kilogram or heist motif)
- Clear, readable typography for card titles and text
- Color-coding or suit system for card categories
- Border/frame design that's consistent across all cards
- Space for card text (complication description and mechanical effect)

### Sample Cards to Illustrate (5 cards recommended)

**1. "Security Guard on Smoke Break"**
- Illustration: Bored security guard outside, creating an opportunity or complication
- Card type: Timing/Personnel complication
- Visual mood: Mundane situation with heist potential

**2. "Laser Grid (Not in the Blueprints)"**
- Illustration: Unexpected security laser grid in a corridor
- Card type: Security/Technical complication
- Visual mood: Modern security meeting unprepared thieves

**3. "The Inside Person is Terrible at This"**
- Illustration: Well-meaning but incompetent inside contact messing something up
- Card type: Ally/Communication complication
- Visual mood: Comedic disaster in progress

**4. "It's Heavier Than Expected"**
- Illustration: Character struggling with the kilogram (only 1kg but psychologically heavy)
- Card type: Physical/Psychological complication
- Visual mood: Absurd situation played seriously

**5. "Mime Troupe Arrives (Wait, You Knew Them?)"**
- Illustration: Helpful mime troupe providing distraction
- Card type: Flashback/Opportunity complication
- Visual mood: Unexpected advantage with comedic energy

### Design Style

**Overall Aesthetic:**
- Professional quality card game design
- Illustrations should be clear and readable at card size
- Balance between comedic content and functional game component
- Icons or symbols to quickly communicate card type/effect
- Color palette consistent with game's overall branding

**Tone:**
Illustrations should capture the comedic heist energy - professional criminals encountering absurd situations and improvising solutions.
