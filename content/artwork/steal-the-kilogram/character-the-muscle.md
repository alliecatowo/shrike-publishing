## Character Role: The Muscle

**Role Description:**
Sometimes the answer is "apply force." The physical specialist who handles obstacles, carries equipment, and provides security for the crew.

### Artwork Requirements

**Character Concept:**
A strong, imposing presence who's equally comfortable lifting heavy objects and intimidating security guards. Power with purpose.

**Visual Elements:**

- Powerful, confident stance or action pose (ready for physical challenge)
- Athletic build suggesting both strength and capability
- Props: duffle bag, crowbar, rope, or communications equipment
- Expression of calm readiness or slight amusement at physical challenges
- Clothing: practical, allows movement, possibly athletic or tactical-inspired

**Personality Through Design:**

- Gender-neutral or can be adapted for any identity
- Body language suggesting controlled power, not aggression
- Looks capable but not stereotypically "tough guy" - this is a professional
- Maybe subverted expectations (reading glasses, a subtle detail suggesting unexpected depth)

**Context:**
This character handles the physical aspects of the heist: moving equipment, dealing with physical obstacles, and ensuring the crew's safety. They're the one who says "I've got this" when something needs to be lifted, moved, or forcibly opened.
