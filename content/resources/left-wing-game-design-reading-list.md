# Left-Wing Game Design Reading List

## Overview

A living document of books, essays, games, and other resources for designers who want to make games about solidarity, systemic change, and collective power—without being preachy or reductive.

## What You'll Find

This isn't just theory—it's theory in conversation with practical game design. Each entry includes:
- Why it matters for game design
- Key concepts that translate to mechanics
- Games that demonstrate these ideas
- Questions to ask about your own work

## Major Sections

### Labor & Work
From union organizing to emotional labor, how do we represent work in games beyond resource extraction?

**Featured texts:**
- Kathi Weeks, *The Problem with Work*
- David Graeber, *Bullshit Jobs*
- Kim Stanley Robinson, *The Ministry for the Future*

### Collective Action
What does solidarity look like as a game mechanic? How do we design cooperation that isn't just "players versus environment"?

**Featured games:**
- *Bloc by Bloc* (board game)
- *Tonight We Riot* (video game)
- *Good Society* (TTRPG)

### Systems Thinking
Understanding capitalism as a system, not a collection of evil individuals. How do we model structural problems?

**Featured texts:**
- Ursula K. Le Guin, *The Dispossessed*
- Octavia Butler, *Parable of the Sower*
- Rebecca Solnit, *A Paradise Built in Hell*

### Alternatives & Futures
Imagining post-capitalist worlds without falling into naive utopianism.

**Featured games:**
- *Dialect* (TTRPG about language and community)
- *The Quiet Year* (map-drawing game)
- *Dream Askew/Dream Apart* (queer community survival)

### Mechanics of Liberation
Specific game mechanics that embody leftist principles: shared resources, collective decision-making, mutual aid, etc.
