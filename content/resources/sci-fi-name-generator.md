# Science Fantasy Name Generator

## Overview

Generate names for your science fantasy worlds that sound plausible, are pronounceable, and don't feel like keyboard-mashing. Perfect for NPCs, planets, starships, and that corporation that definitely isn't evil (but totally is).

## Features

### Character Names
- Human names with cultural mixing (because the future is syncretic)
- Alien names with consistent phonetic patterns
- Nicknames and callsigns
- Full name + title generators

### Place Names
- Planets and moons
- Space stations and habitats
- Cities and settlements
- Geographic features

### Ship Names
- Corporate vessels (sterile and bureaucratic)
- Military ships (dramatic and intimidating)
- Independent traders (quirky and personal)
- Generation ships (hopeful and ominous)

### Organization Names
- Corporations (evil and/or banal)
- Resistance movements (aspirational)
- Guilds and unions
- Religious/philosophical orders

## How It Works

Unlike AI generators that produce unpronounceable garbage, this tool uses:
- **Phonetic pattern libraries** from real languages
- **Customizable syllable structures** for consistency
- **Cultural mixing algorithms** for realistic diaspora naming
- **Semantic meaning options** (optional - for when you want names to mean something)

## Settings & Customization

- Adjust syllable count (2-5)
- Choose phonetic complexity (simple to complex)
- Set cultural influences (East Asian, Latinate, Arabic, Synthetic, etc.)
- Generate in batches (10-50 at once)
- Export to text or JSON

## Use Cases

- "I need 20 NPC names for tonight's game session"

- "I'm worldbuilding and need consistent place names for an entire solar system"
