# Solarpunk Worldbuilding Toolkit

## Overview

Building solarpunk worlds is harder than it looks. You want hope without naiveté, technology without techno-optimism, ecology without primitivism. This toolkit helps you navigate those tensions.

## What's Inside

- **20-page PDF guide** with frameworks and prompts
- **Society-building templates** for post-scarcity communities
- **Technology audit worksheet** - what survives the collapse and why
- **Conflict generators** that don't rely on dystopia
- **Bibliography** of solarpunk theory and fiction

## Who It's For

- TTRPG game masters building hopeful futures
- Fiction writers tired of apocalypse porn
- Game designers exploring alternative futures
- Anyone who wants to imagine better worlds without ignoring the struggle to get there

## Key Topics Covered

### The Three Tensions
How to balance utopia/dystopia, tradition/progress, and individual/collective without falling into false dichotomies.

### Technology After Collapse
What happens to the internet when the grid goes down? How do communities maintain medical tech? When does low-tech make sense and when is it just aesthetic?

### Practical Utopianism
Building societies that acknowledge human nature, resource constraints, and historical memory while still reaching for something better.

### Conflict Without Villains
Generating dramatic tension from systemic problems, competing values, and the friction of building new worlds.
