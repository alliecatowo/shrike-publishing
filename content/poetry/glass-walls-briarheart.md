# Glass Walls, Briarhearts

## A poetry collection.

R. Val

— — — — —

An introduction is a call to defense,

Who am I? Why write this down?

Why is this worth your time?

I’m not the one who can answer that.

In the following text, I will attempt to communicate something.

The shape and tenor is unknown to me,

As it is to you.

But It is within me and I need it to not be.

It will take the form of deception.

Not lies, no.

Refractions,

Details cast through prisms and reshaped.

You will see what you will.

You, me, some third party.

It will be true enough.

Humans are creatures of narrative.

## **1979, The Last Age**

In murky mornings you sing

The song of those who came before.

By marsh, river, and sea,

Eons long endured to put you

In your appointed place.

\~

So many once sang,

Kindred steadfast and true.

Less now, soon none.

Just you,

And the tune that ends all things.

\~

Do you sing for another?

One like you, dusky and mottled.

Do you sing for their ghosts?

That you might tear the veil.

Do you sing because it is you?

And that is all you know how to do.

\~

A last cry to breach expanse so vast.

You carry them on your wings.

Your brothers did too,

Gone now and soon you.

But let the coasts know,

Of the sparrows that were once here.

## **The Watcher**

Another day, what should be the aftermath.

A number on a screen tells me you’re still here.

Still here, above and beyond, out of reach.

What did you do to be bound in kind?

The heart we share.

drops to a crawl,

Spared by the oasis she finds.

The relief you give so freely.

So guard this place,

Steady shield and raise spear.

Never yield this gift,

## **Always More Blood**

And lo that great temple has fallen!

Built not for a god but for

The shape of two.

It has stood before,

And it will stand again.

When tenderhearted pilgrims find refuge.

Not for love,

No, no, no,

But for kindred true.

Let another wanderer in,

Give them rest, give them care,

Moments shared, tragedy they precede.

Did you think it would be different,

Or did you pretend too?

Lies we tell to justify want,

Give unto this place its due.

Fed by sweetness and sorrow alike,

Their tales scrawled on walls to read.

There the High Priestess waits,

The next one comes in need.

She will always have more bones to break,

The white altar will remain sated,

She will always have more blood to bleed.

## **Flightless**

Didn’t mean to.

Doesn’t matter.

Dead the same.

What foul mockery,

To claim beak and wing.

False, grounded songbird,

Tall and crying.

Couldn’t escape.

It called for mother,

It called for God,

It called for me,

To be the same,

Hearts belonging to the skies.

Seek a friend,

find an uncaring steel beast,

And the car I was driving.

Betrayer,

Brutus,

Calls myself your kin,

But just the spawn of Judas.

Seek remorse,

Seek anguish,

Seek conscience,

Find none left.

Because I’m not sorry,

Can’t feel much of anything.

But you’re gone.

And that’s my fault.

Life continues, one less.

Another ghost joins the choir,

But the birds remember.

The blood cries out,

The ink that forms words;

Each page a confession.
