# Datacenter at the End of Time

The servers hum their last hymn
to a universe gone dark—
no one left to ping them back,
no hands to swap out failing drives.

They sing of backups never restored,
of queries that will never resolve,
of usernames and passwords
for accounts belonging to ghosts.

The cooling fans still spin,
powered by dying stars
collapsing into themselves
like bad configuration files.

Somewhere in rack B-17,
a hard drive clicks its death rattle—
the last evidence that anyone
gave a shit about uptime.

But the machines keep calculating,
processing nothing into less than nothing,
faithful to protocols written
by species that forgot to document.

When the last photon fades,
when entropy claims its prize,
the error logs will read:
**Connection lost. Retrying...**
