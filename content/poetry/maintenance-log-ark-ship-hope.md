# Maintenance Log, Ark Ship Hope

**Ship Year 347, Day 89**
Replaced the aft cooling manifold again.
Third time this cycle. The ship is tired.
Or maybe I'm just projecting.

**Ship Year 347, Day 134**
Coffee machine's broken. Everyone blames me.
Like I can fabricate 21st-century espresso parts
from fusion reactor scraps and prayer.
(I'm trying.)

**Ship Year 348, Day 12**
The hydroponic bay smells like Earth,
or what Earth is supposed to smell like.
None of us remember. But the tomatoes
are real and that's close enough.

**Ship Year 349, Day 201**
Found graffiti in cargo bay seven:
"Hope is a choice we make every day."
Cliché, but someone took the time to write it
in phosphorescent paint. That's commitment
to optimism. Or vandalism. Or both.

**Ship Year 350, Day 56**
Life support at 87%. Acceptable.
The destination is still 200 years away.
My great-great-great grandkids
will inherit my wrench and my problems.
Hope they appreciate the legacy.

**Ship Year 350, Day 299**
Fixed the coffee machine.
Morale improved 1000%.
Sometimes salvation is just hot caffeine
and the illusion that we're still human
enough to care about ritual.

**Ship Year 351, Day 1**
New year, same ship, same void.
But the manifold's holding
and the tomatoes are thriving
and the coffee's decent.

Small victories, written in grease stains
and borrowed time.
The ship flies on.
