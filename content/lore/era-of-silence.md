# The Era of Silence

The Era of Silence marks the beginning of a new dark age. On the day later known as **The Falling**, all forms of long-distance communication ceased simultaneously across the globe. Radio waves died. Internet connections vanished. Even simple telecommunications became impossible.

## What Happened

No one knows for certain what caused The Falling. Theories range from solar events to deliberate sabotage, from alien intervention to a fundamental shift in physics itself. What is known is that on that fateful day, humanity was plunged into isolation.

### Immediate Aftermath

- **First Week**: Panic spread as cities realized they were cut off from the outside world
- **First Month**: Supply chains collapsed as coordination became impossible
- **First Year**: Governments fell as nations fragmented into isolated regions
- **First Decade**: New societies emerged from the ruins, adapted to a world of silence

## The World Today

Centuries have passed since The Falling. Society has rebuilt, but in a fundamentally different way. Communication is limited to what can be carried by messenger or seen with the naked eye. Cities are smaller, more self-sufficient. The world has become a collection of isolated pockets of humanity.

## Mysteries Remain

Strange phenomena persist in the world of silence:
- **The Void**: Areas of complete sensory deprivation that seem to spread slowly
- **The Whispers**: Rare instances where people claim to hear distant voices
- **The Seekers**: Those who claim they can restore communication and seek ancient technology
