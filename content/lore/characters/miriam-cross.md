# Miriam Cross

Miriam Cross is perhaps the most documented and reliable Void Walker in the Eastern Territories. With over 200 successful Void crossings and zero casualties, she has become both a legend and a source of hope for communities separated by expanding Void zones.

## Background

Little is known about Miriam's early life. She first appeared in the records of Haven's Rest approximately fifteen years ago, offering her services as a guide through a particularly troublesome Void zone that had cut the settlement off from its trading partners.

### First Recorded Crossing
Her first documented crossing involved leading a merchant caravan of twelve people through what was believed to be an impassable Void zone. The journey took three days, and all emerged unharmed, though clearly shaken by the experience.

## Appearance

Miriam is described consistently across accounts:
- Tall, around six feet
- Dark hair with premature silver streaks (she appears to be in her 40s)
- Distinctive spiral scars on both forearms
- Always wears a long coat with numerous pockets
- Carries an unusual compass that she claims helps her navigate the Void

## Methods

Unlike many Void Walkers who refuse to discuss their techniques, Miriam is somewhat more forthcoming:

### Her Principles
1. **Never rush**: A Void crossing cannot be hurried
2. **Trust the silence**: Fighting the sensory deprivation makes it worse
3. **Follow the currents**: She describes "flows" within the Void that safe paths follow
4. **Preparation is survival**: She insists on specific preparations before any crossing

### Preparation Requirements
Before accepting a crossing contract, Miriam requires:
- Three days of fasting for all participants
- Sensory deprivation training
- Mental preparation exercises
- Specific equipment (rope, bells, marked stones)

## Notable Achievements

### The Haven's Rest Corridor
Established a permanent marked route through a major Void zone, enabling regular trade

### The Children of Millbrook
Successfully evacuated forty-three children from a settlement threatened by an expanding Void

### The Lost Expedition Recovery
Retrieved the remains and journals of a failed exploration team from deep within a Void zone

## Controversies

Despite her success rate, Miriam is not without critics:
- Some claim her "perfect record" is maintained by refusing difficult crossings
- Questions persist about how she gained her abilities
- Her high fees are seen by some as exploitation
- Rumors suggest she has ties to the mysterious Silent Brotherhood

## Current Status

Miriam continues to operate from her base in Haven's Rest. She accepts approximately one contract per month, carefully selecting missions she believes she can complete safely. Her services are expensive but considered worth the cost by those desperate enough to need them.

## Philosophy

In one rare interview, Miriam shared her thoughts on the Void:

> "People fear the Void because it takes away everything they rely on. But silence isn't empty—it's full of things we've forgotten how to hear. I don't conquer the Void. I listen to it, and it shows me the way."

## Contact

Miriam can be reached through the Guild Hall in Haven's Rest. Expect a waiting period of several weeks to months depending on demand.
