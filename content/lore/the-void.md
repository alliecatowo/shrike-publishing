# The Void

The Void represents one of the greatest mysteries of the Era of Silence. These are zones—sometimes mere meters across, sometimes spanning kilometers—where all sensory input simply ceases to exist.

## Characteristics

### What Happens Inside

When entering a Void zone, victims report:
- Complete darkness, regardless of light sources
- Total silence, even one's own heartbeat becomes inaudible
- Loss of all tactile sensation
- Disorientation and panic
- Time dilation effects

### Known Patterns

The Void zones exhibit several consistent behaviors:
- They appear suddenly, without warning
- They expand slowly but inexorably
- They never shrink or disappear
- They seem to avoid areas of high human activity (but this may be observational bias)
- Electronic devices fail completely within Void boundaries

## Theories

### The Continuation Theory
Some believe the Void is the same phenomenon that caused The Falling, continuing to spread and consume reality itself.

### The Barrier Hypothesis
Others theorize that Void zones are protective barriers, shielding certain areas from an unknown external threat.

### The Reversal Doctrine
A minority view suggests that Void zones are actually the "natural" state, and our sensory-rich reality is the anomaly.

## The Void Walkers

A rare few individuals claim to be able to navigate Void zones safely. Called **Void Walkers**, they are either viewed as heroes who can map safe routes around expanding Voids, or as charlatans taking advantage of desperate people.

## Current Status

Approximately 3% of the known world is now consumed by Void zones. At current expansion rates, some scholars predict catastrophic consequences within the next century, though others argue the expansion is slowing.

## Safety Guidelines

If you encounter a Void zone:
1. **Mark it clearly** - Use visible markers to warn others
2. **Report it** - Inform your local authorities immediately
3. **Stay clear** - Maintain a safe distance of at least 50 meters
4. **Never enter** - Even experienced Void Walkers face significant risk
