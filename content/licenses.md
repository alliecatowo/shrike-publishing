title: Licensing - Shrike Publishing
description: Learn about licensing options for creating third-party content for
  Era of Silence, Blood Neon, and other Shrike Publishing games.
faq:
  - label: Do I need to pay royalties to use your third-party licenses?
    content: No! Our third-party licenses are free to use. You can publish and sell
      your content without paying us royalties. We only ask that you follow the
      license requirements and give proper credit.
    defaultOpen: true
  - label: Can I use artwork from your official books?
    content: Official artwork, logos, and trademarked elements are not covered under
      the third-party licenses. If you need to use these, please contact us for
      custom licensing arrangements.
  - label: What platforms can I sell my third-party content on?
    content: You can sell your licensed content on any platform you choose,
      including Itch.io, DriveThruRPG, your own website, or through crowdfunding
      platforms like Kickstarter.
  - label: Can I create content in other languages?
    content: Yes! Translations and content in any language are welcome under our
      third-party licenses. We love seeing our games reach global audiences.
  - label: How do I get my content featured on your website?
    content: We love featuring community content! Reach out to us through our
      contact page with information about your project. We regularly spotlight
      third-party content on our blog and social media.
  - label: What if I want to use your system for a different setting?
    content: You can use mechanics and system elements under our licenses, but if
      you want to create a substantially different game or setting, please
      contact us to discuss custom licensing options.

  #### Here’s everything you need to know to make your own Era of Silence module, digital tool, peripheral, new game based on ours, and anything else! There will be a fair amount of legal speak that you need to read but we try to keep the speech as plain as possible.
  
  Still read on, but in summary:
  
  - You can make anything you want with Era of Silence’s text (not the art), but you have to credit the original game and Shrike Publishing.
  - If you design under this license the thing you produce has to be available under this license.
  
  ## **The License**
  
  The written content of Era of Silence, including but not limited to text and game mechanics, is offered under a CC BY-SA 4.0 license. This means you are free to distribute the text and to adapt it within the scope of the license, which requires that you give Shrike Publishing credit and that you share your adaptations under similar terms. Adaptations include, but are not limited to, web tools for character creation or other software tools designed to work with Era of Silence.
  
  If you create an adaptation of Era of Silence, you must credit Era of Silence and the publisher, Shrike Publishing. However, if the Adapted Material is a literary work (such as an RPG book), then the Adapter’s License need only be applied to the text (not, for instance, illustrations). Additionally, you must include this and the following sentence in your licensing terms: 'This work is based on or includes material from Era of Silence, © Shrike Publishing, used under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Adapted Material is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.' The CC BY-SA 4.0 license is available at creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
