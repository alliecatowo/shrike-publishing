    content: "Official artwork, logos, and trademarked elements are not covered under the third-party licenses. If you need to use these, please contact us for custom licensing arrangements."

At Shrike Publishing, we encourage creativity and collaboration. We offer several licensing options to help you create content compatible with our games or use our systems in your own projects. Whether you're a content creator, publisher, or hobbyist, we have options for you.

::u-page-section{class="max-w-5xl mx-auto"}
:::license-card{title="Era of Silence Third-Party License" icon="i-lucide-file-text" badgeText="Open License" badgeColor="success" borderColor="primary" iconColor="primary" buttonText="Learn About Era of Silence" buttonUrl="/games/era-of-silence" buttonIcon="i-lucide-book-open"}
Create and publish your own content for Era of Silence under our third-party license.

#### What You Can Do:
- Create adventures, campaigns, and supplements for Era of Silence
- Publish and sell your content on platforms like Itch.io and DriveThruRPG
- Use our game mechanics and setting elements in your work
- Reference official Era of Silence materials in your products

#### Requirements:
- Include the Era of Silence Third-Party License notice in your product
- Clearly indicate your product is not official Shrike Publishing content
- Do not use our trademarked logos or official artwork without permission
- Maintain the spirit and tone of the Era of Silence setting

#### License Text:
```
[Product Name] is an independent production by [Author/Publisher Name] and is not affiliated with Shrike Publishing.
It is published under the Era of Silence Third-Party License.
Era of Silence is copyright Shrike Publishing.
```
:::

:::license-card{title="Blood Neon Compatibility License" icon="i-lucide-zap" badgeText="Open License" badgeColor="pink" borderColor="pink" iconColor="pink" buttonText="Learn About Blood Neon" buttonUrl="/games/blood-neon" buttonIcon="i-lucide-zap"}
Design cyberpunk content compatible with Blood Neon's fast-paced system.

#### What You Can Do:
- Create adventures, characters, and gear for Blood Neon
- Design new corporations, factions, and districts
- Publish commercial or free content under this license
- Adapt Blood Neon mechanics for your own cyberpunk settings

#### Requirements:
- Include the Blood Neon Compatibility License notice
- State clearly that your product requires Blood Neon Core Rules
- Do not reproduce the core mechanics verbatim without permission
- Credit Shrike Publishing as the creator of Blood Neon

#### License Text:
```
[Product Name] is an independent production by [Author/Publisher Name] and is not affiliated with Shrike Publishing.
It is compatible with Blood Neon and published under the Blood Neon Compatibility License.
Blood Neon is copyright Shrike Publishing.
```
:::

Need more flexibility or want to use our IP in unique ways? Let's talk.

#### Custom Arrangements For:
- Large-scale commercial projects or translations
- Use of official artwork, logos, or trademarked elements
- Creating derivative games based on our systems
- Digital adaptations (video games, apps, etc.)
- Podcast, streaming, or video content partnerships

We're open to creative partnerships and are happy to work with you to find an arrangement that benefits both parties. Every project is unique, and we approach each opportunity with flexibility and enthusiasm.
:::

:::license-card{title="Fan Content Policy" icon="i-lucide-heart" badgeText="Always Allowed" badgeColor="success" borderColor="green" iconColor="success"}
We love seeing what the community creates! Here's what you can do without asking.

#### You're Always Welcome To:
- Share actual play videos, podcasts, and streams
- Create fan art, fiction, and character builds
- Design and share homebrew content for personal use
- Write reviews, guides, and tutorials
- Share your experiences on social media

#### We Just Ask That You:
- Make it clear you're a fan, not officially affiliated
- Don't use our content in ways that could harm others
- Tag us so we can see and share your awesome work!
:::
